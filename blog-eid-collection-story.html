The Eid Collection Story

This year's Eid collection started with a single embroidered sample from a small workshop outside Lahore — hand needlework we hadn't seen used on ready-to-wear pieces before.

We built the rest of the collection around that technique: kurta sets and gharara pieces where the embroidery is placed by hand, piece by piece, rather than machine-repeated across a bolt of fabric. It takes longer, and it's why this collection runs in smaller batches than usual.

Sourcing every metre

Every fabric in the collection was sourced directly from mills we've worked with for past seasons, so we could confirm colourfastness and hand-feel before committing to the run.

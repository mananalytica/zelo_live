About Zelo

The online and live-sell storefront of Zainab Export — genuine export-leftover fashion, straight from the warehouse floor.

Zelo is where Zainab Export sells directly to shoppers — both online through this website and live, in real time, over video. We stream from our warehouse floor so you can see every piece up close, ask questions in the chat, and buy the moment you spot something you like.

What we sell

Our catalogue covers four core categories, all genuine export-leftover stock at a fraction of retail price:

  • Stitched clothes — ready-to-wear suits and outfits, finished and ready to ship.
  • Unstitched fabric — export-quality unstitched suit pieces, sold by the set so you can have them tailored to fit.
  • Laces & trims — embroidered and embellished laces sold live, lot by lot, so you can see the exact roll before you buy.
  • Patches — decorative and embroidered patches for finishing and embellishing your own pieces.

How Live Sell works

During a Live Sell stream, our team shows each piece on camera, calls out the price, and takes orders in the order they're claimed in chat. It's the closest thing to shopping the warehouse floor in person — without leaving home.

Where we are

We're based in Gulberg Industrial Area, Lahore, and ship across Pakistan with Cash on Delivery available on every order.

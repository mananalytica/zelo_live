Behind the Seams

Every made-to-measure order at Zelo starts the same way: a set of six numbers, saved once to your account, and a bolt of fabric that hasn't been cut yet.

From there it's about twelve hours of hands-on work spread across three days — pattern drafting against your saved measurements, a first cut, an internal fit check, and finishing. Nothing leaves the studio floor until it's been checked against the numbers you gave us, not a size chart.

Why we don't use standard sizing

Standard sizing solves for an average that doesn't describe anyone in particular. Working from your own measurements means the piece fits the way you actually stand, not the way a size label assumes you do.

What this means for Live Sell

Export-leftover and overrun pieces sold through Live Sell are, by definition, already cut — so we're upfront about sizing on every lot, and our team is in the live chat to answer fit questions before you buy.

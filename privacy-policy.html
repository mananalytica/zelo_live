Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

This policy explains what information Zelo ("we", "us") collects when you browse the site, place an order, book a fitting, or watch a Live Sell stream, and how that information is used.

What we collect

Contact details you provide (name, phone, address, email); order and measurement details for made-to-measure pieces; and, where you've accepted cookies, anonymised usage data via our analytics provider (RudderStack) to understand how the Shop and Live Sell pages are used.

How we use it

To fulfil and deliver orders, confirm bookings, respond to enquiries, and improve the site. We do not sell your personal information.

Cash on Delivery orders

For Live Sell and Shop orders placed with Cash on Delivery, your name, phone number, and address are shared only with our delivery partner for that order.

Your choices

You can decline non-essential cookies from the consent banner at any time, and can request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us — see Contact.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@zelo.pk.

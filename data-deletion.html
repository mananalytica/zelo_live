Data Deletion

Last updated: 5 August 2026

You can request that Zelo ("we", "us") delete the personal data we hold about you at any time — whether you signed up on the website, placed an order, or connected with us through a linked service such as Facebook or Instagram.

What gets deleted

Your account details, saved contact information, and order history associated with your account or phone number. If you've placed a Cash on Delivery order, we may be required to retain basic order records for a limited period for accounting and legal purposes before final deletion.

How to request deletion

Send a deletion request using any of the methods below. Please include the name, email, or phone number associated with your account so we can locate your data.

  • Email us at sardarrobinaniaz@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  • Message us on +92 321 4857054.
  • Use the Contact form and select "Data Deletion" in your message.

What happens next

We'll confirm receipt of your request and complete the deletion within 30 days, then send you a confirmation once it's done.

Facebook / Instagram login users

If you signed in or connected via Facebook or Instagram, you can also remove Zelo's access directly from your Facebook account: go to Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites, find Zelo, and select Remove. This revokes our access; you can still use the methods above to request deletion of any data already stored with us.

Contact

Questions about this process can be sent to sardarrobinaniaz@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
